GOVERNMENT OF MADHYA PRADESH
DEPARTMENT OF MINORITY WELFARE & BACKWARD CLASSES
MANTRALAYA, BHOPAL

Circular No. MW/2026/781/SCH                                     Date: 20th March 2026

OFFICIAL GAZETTE NOTIFICATION

Subject: Revision of Annual Income Threshold for Post-Matric Minority Scholarship Scheme.

Clause 1: Background & Objective
In pursuance of the State Cabinet Decision dated 15th March 2026, the Department hereby updates the income ceiling for financial aid grants across recognized colleges.

Clause 2: Revision Provision
Clause 3.2: The annual family income threshold for post-matric minority scholarships is hereby revised from Rs. 2,00,000 (Two Lakhs) to Rs. 2,50,000 (Two Lakh Fifty Thousand) effective Academic Year 2026-27.

By Order,
Director of Minority Welfare, MP
